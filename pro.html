SettleClear Pro — planned, not yet available

The core SettleClear tool — true profit per SKU and a fee audit from your Amazon settlement, entirely in your browser — is free and stays free. This page describes what a paid Pro tier would add. It does not exist yet: there is no signup and no charge today. It is here so the roadmap is honest and public.

What Pro would add

  • Multi-platform — parse Walmart and Temu settlements alongside Amazon, in one profit view. (Today the free tool parses Amazon Flat File V2 only; see the Walmart and Temu fee guides.)
  • Saved history — keep a dated record of every settlement so you build the long trend Amazon only retains for ~90 days, without re-uploading.
  • Continuous fee-overcharge monitoring — flag suspicious fees and missing reimbursements automatically each settlement, not just when you remember to check (see appealing overcharges).
  • Batch and export — multiple accounts, scheduled exports, and bookkeeper-ready journals (see QuickBooks/Xero reconciliation).

What would stay free

The single-settlement, in-browser profit and audit tool — the part most sellers need — remains free and open source, with your data never leaving your machine. Pro would be for sellers who want history, automation, and more than one marketplace.

For now, the honest answer is: use the free tool. If you want to be told when Pro is real, the button above appears once a waitlist is open.
