Why your Seller Central number isn't your profit

Amazon deducts 40+ fee types from every payout — referral fees, FBA fulfillment, weight handling, monthly and long-term storage, refund administration, and advertising you paid separately. The summary shows revenue; the settlement report holds the real deductions. SettleClear reads the settlement line by line, classifies every fee, joins your cost of goods, and shows what you actually keep per SKU — then flags the rows that don't reconcile so a clean-looking sheet never hides a silent gap.

  1. Download the Flat File V2 settlement report from Seller Central.
  2. Drop it in — parsing happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
  3. Add COGS (optional) to turn gross into true net profit per SKU.
  4. Review the audit — negative-margin SKUs and unclassified fee lines are flagged.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. SettleClear is 100% client-side JavaScript. Your settlement file is parsed in your own browser and never sent to any server. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Which report do I upload?

The Amazon Flat File V2 Settlement Report: Seller Central → Reports → Payments → Date Range Reports. It is tab- or comma-delimited (.txt / .csv). SettleClear auto-detects the delimiter.

How is true profit calculated?

Net proceeds per SKU = product charges − Amazon fees (referral, FBA, storage, refund admin, etc.) − promotional rebates − refunds + reimbursements. If you add per-unit COGS, that is subtracted to give net profit. Every number traces back to lines in your own report.

What does the fee audit flag?

Rows where order and settlement amounts don't reconcile, fee lines the parser can't classify (so they're never silently averaged away), and SKUs that lose money once all fees and COGS are counted.

Does it cost anything?

The tool is free. It's open source — you can read exactly what it does on GitHub.
